BotOffice · Google Ads

A platform with Google Ads management capabilities.

BotOffice lets ad managers and agencies manage their own Google Ads accounts and the client accounts they run, all in one place, with a private AI assistant that knows their business and helps them decide what to change.

What it does with Google Ads

Performance reporting

See spend, clicks, conversions and cost per conversion across accounts, campaigns, ad groups and keywords, with alerts on what changed.

Campaign management

Adjust budgets, bids, targeting and the on or off status of campaigns and ads from one place.

Campaign creation

Set up new Search, Performance Max, Display, Demand Gen and Video campaigns, ad groups, keywords and ads.

Keyword planning

Get keyword ideas, search volume and forecasts to plan campaigns before they go live.

How it works

  1. You connect your Google Ads accounts, or the client accounts you manage, through Google's standard sign-in.
  2. BotOffice reads the account's performance data so you and the assistant can see how things are going.
  3. The private AI assistant learns your business and suggests budgets, bids, keywords and campaigns to adjust or create.
  4. Every change is shown to you and applied only after you confirm it. You stay in control of the account.

For agencies and ad managers

Manage every client account under one roof. Client accounts are linked under our manager account, so you can report on and manage them all from BotOffice.

We do not resell Google Ads data and we do not use it for competitor research. You can disconnect any account at any time.
